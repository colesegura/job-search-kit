# Understand the person before choosing the job title

The starting unit is a desired working week and life arrangement. A person who loves photography may want ordinary work that protects their creative energy, rather than a photography job. A person who mentions a ranch may want practical learning, community and affordable living rather than livestock duties.

## Source scouting

First ask which sources the user wants to use and what may be accessed: an existing resume, selected conversations, work samples, prior applications, portfolios, certificates, calendars, or specific recruiter threads. Inventory the named sources before asking the same questions again. Do not assume access to all browser tabs, chats, drives or accounts. A private source export belongs in private/; record its path and provenance instead of copying it into public files.

Extract two separate sets:

- **Verified experience:** role, dates, actual responsibilities, outcomes the user can explain, certifications and expiration, work rights, relevant practical constraints. An artifact shows something was made, not that the applicant personally performed every part.
- **Preference signals:** what energizes or drains the person, preferred daily activities, schedule, geography, community, learning, compensation, urgency and reversibility. Record user statements separately from your interpretation. Silence is not agreement.

If sources contradict, retain the conflict and ask only if the next action depends on it. Public portfolios can substantiate work samples; they cannot verify private legal or medical answers. Do not scan confidential employer sources for material to export into a personal search.

## A small conversation, then real examples

Begin with two or three questions chosen from what is still unknown:

- What should be different about your ordinary Tuesday, and what should work make possible outside work?
- How soon do you need paid work, and what financial commitments must it cover?
- Which constraints are truly hard, and which would you trade for a compelling arrangement?
- Do you want progression in this work, a temporary bridge, a new place/community, or several possibilities still open?

Discuss sourcing and external-action authority separately from these values questions. Do not make the user complete a long intake form or disclose sensitive facts before a form needs them.

Write a brief in private/current.md with the intended change, requirements, preferences, live hypotheses, rejected interpretations, and next unresolved decision. Each significant criterion in search.json needs an ID, its user's statement or source, and a disposition: `hard`, `preference`, `hypothesis`, `rejected`, or `deferred`.

Translate abstract preferences into observable tests. “Freedom” might mean predictable off days, low after-hours demands, autonomy during work, or a short commitment. These are different arrangements. Ask which interpretation fits, then use actual schedules to test it. Do not assume safety-critical driving or equipment operation permits divided attention.

Bring back a small contrasting set: an accessible bridge, a high-fit stretch, and an unexpected mechanism if evidence supports them. Ask what attracts or repels the user about the actual duties and terms. Use the reaction to revise the brief, not to select a permanent identity.

## Discussion prerequisites

Before broad research: enough understanding to choose a useful bounded comparison, permitted sources, and known hard constraints.

Before applications: verified candidate facts, a truthful material baseline, desired application scope, availability, and transmission authority. Ask only the sensitive questions actually required by the selected forms.

Before creative or create-the-role outreach: a specific buyer/employer need, a substantiated offer the user wants to fulfill, relevant proof, and one accepted example. Volume cannot substitute for establishing the proposition.

Before acceptance or relocation: confirmed terms, realistic finances, housing/transport continuity, schedule, commitment and unresolved tradeoffs. No research score can decide those for the user.
