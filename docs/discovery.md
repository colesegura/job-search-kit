# Search several ways, with an inspectable frontier

Use a bounded search brief derived from the user's actual preferences. The comparison unit is employer + role or proposal + location/living arrangement + workweek + compensation + entry route + start window. A prestigious name or scenic photograph does not establish a good arrangement.

## Methods and when to switch

| Method | Useful for | Main failure | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Role and keyword matrix | Fast initial discovery and urgent local hiring | Titles miss adjacent duties; boards repeat | Expand title synonyms and verify official listings |
| Employer ecosystems | Niche operations, apprenticeships and hidden entry paths | Directory membership mistaken for hiring | Enumerate employers, inspect careers/ATS, label unadvertised routes |
| Place and catchment inventory | Place-led lives and regional completeness | Famous places crowd out gateways; a map becomes fake coverage | Use actual hiring/commute catchments and dated source logs |
| Schedule and economics retrieval | Rotations, concentrated weeks, housing-backed work | Advertised benefits become guaranteed savings | Search schedule language, verify hours, costs and continuity |
| Experience adjacency | Unfamiliar work with credible entry paths | Transferable skills inflated into credentials | Distinguish evidence, prerequisites and trainable gaps |
| Future/general application and need-led proposals | Organizations without a suitable current vacancy | A general contact becomes a claimed job | Label the entry route and substantiate the actual need |
| Warm-thread recovery | Converting existing employer interest | Interview invitations counted as offers or completed portals | Recover exact next step and its evidence |

Use geography appropriate to the user: commute corridors, countries/work-right regimes, industry clusters, employer networks or landscape regions. Ecoregions are optional. Do not ship one person's map, preferences or employer list as the default for everyone.

Translate each lens into concrete role vocabulary, employer families and source routes. Search job boards/directories for discovery; follow promising results to employer pages or official ATS. Use official eligibility sources when needed. Worker accounts can signal conditions worth checking, but do not establish role-specific terms. Search results and old pages may be stale; missing or failed sources are not negative findings.

On a saturated second batch, change retrieval methods. Report each result as net-new, materially updated or duplicate. Do not repeat the same queries and call it progress.

## Search waves and agent assignments

Start with a few contrasting actual arrangements. Broaden only as the brief and urgency warrant. Keep near-term applications moving if separately authorized; exploratory discovery can continue beside them.

When delegation is authorized, partition by concrete catchment, employer family or source method. Each assignment states the user brief, exact inventory, owned output file, deadline/budget, sources to check, required fields, previous-ledger deduplication and research-only boundary. Workers are not alone; they must not revert others' work. Root owns synthesis and the shared ledger. Check actual agent/tool availability and run sequentially if unavailable; report this honestly.

Before dispatching applications, test computer-use access for the actual executor. A researcher with no browser access can prepare an application packet, but cannot submit it. One operator owns a shared mailbox or browser mutation surface; disjoint tabs alone do not make shared login/session state independent.

Each returned package must include sources, structured leads, inventory touched, failed routes and remaining gaps. A malformed or missing package is a gap to repair. Agents' summaries are signals until evidence is reviewed.

## Coverage that means something

In the tracker use `unsearched`, `partial`, or `screened`. Each unit identifies its actual scope, required source families, checked sources, checked time and remaining gaps. Only `screened` means the disclosed finite procedure was completed. A generated place × family × route matrix proves registration, not search completion or a census of all employers.

After a wave, check untouched categories, neglected entry routes, stale sources, too-narrow eligibility assumptions and plausible wild cards. Define the endpoint as completed passes against a named inventory plus an explicit remaining frontier. Do not claim global exhaustiveness.

## Compare without hiding the field

For each materially relevant option record actual duties, pay/currency, known hours, tips/bonus assumptions, deductions, housing terms and allocation, schedule, transport, start/end, requirements, eligibility, source dates, route, uncertainties and history.

Keep `fit`, `feasibility`, and `confidence` as separate prose judgments. Do not invent numerical offer probabilities. Housing advertised for an employer does not establish a bed for this role. An employer's package valuation is not cash pay. If calculating finances, separate guaranteed cash from estimated tips and supplied benefits; show costs and assumptions. Unknown does not mean zero.

Present a browsable field with recommendations layered on top. Exclude for a demonstrated hard conflict, closed route or unreliable evidence, and record why. Keep future cycles, trainee inquiries and general contacts in labeled sections. Let the user see surprising options you would not personally rank first.
